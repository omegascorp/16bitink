[dramatically] Round one is simple. Stop swimming. For five minutes. [chuckles] Easy, right? [nervous] Not for everyone. Some fish can only breathe while they move: water has to rush in through the mouth and out over the gills. [surprised] So when the tunas stop… skipjack, yellowfin, bluefin… ninety of them are out! [shocked] And then… the great white. [dramatically] The great white shark is OUT. In round one! [curious] Scientists class it as a shark that has to keep swimming to breathe, so a great white that stops… is in trouble. [sarcastic] The mako? Same problem, say the books. Out. [warmly] And before you type it: no, not every shark has to swim. A nurse shark can lie on the bottom and pump water itself. [relieved] Everyone else pumps water over their own gills… and just waits.
