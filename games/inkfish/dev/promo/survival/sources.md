# Fish Survival Tournament: source pull (2026-11-12)

Method: web search + page fetches this session. Several journal pages (JEB, Springer, PubMed, Nature, ResearchGate) returned 403/redirects, so some numbers come from abstracts or reputable secondary summaries; each row says which. "Not seen" = I could not open the paper itself. Units: US first, metric in brackets. 100 F = 37.8 C.

Stamps: MEASURED (in a study) / ESTIMATED / REPORTED (review, press, anecdote).

## Final head-to-head: proposal

Best defensible: **days out of water in damp air**.
- Rivulus: 66 days (1,584 h), lab, damp mud, starving (Taylor 1990). MEASURED. A Wright-lab "85+ days" quote is available as a comment-bait aside only.
- Mudskipper: "at least 2 days (48 h) without dying, plus a 2.5-day quote; nobody has measured its limit" (Ip/Chew; Gordon). This is a floor.
Caution: framed as "66 vs 2" it is real but lopsided; stamp the mudskipper figure as a lower bound and say so. A fair story beat: the mudskipper is the fan favourite that LOOKS terrestrial but is a tidal commuter, rivulus is the quiet monster.

Alternative final that is cleaner on both sides: **salinity**. Rivulus wild 0-70 ppt, lab adapted to 114 ppt (King 1989, via Taylor 2012). Mudskipper P. minutus no deaths at 200% sea water (~70 ppt), burrow 84 psu, but half died in fresh water in 4 days. That is a real two-number duel, but the mudskipper then loses on being freshwater-intolerant, which is also species-specific (Periophthalmus vs Boleophthalmus differ). Use only with the species named.

## 10-line summary of changes vs research.md

1. 66 days is Taylor 1990, lab, damp mud, emaciated fish, not Abel 1987 (that is 30+ days). Drop "wild" implication; "85+ days" is only a lab-head quote.
2. Rivulus CTmax is a loss-of-equilibrium of ~40.5-41.5 C (105-107 F), derived by me from the text; verify Fig 1 before a decimal.
3. Rivulus salt: wild 0-70 ppt, lab 70-80, adapted to 114 ppt; "abrupt" in Sutton 2018 means 10/20/30 ppt jumps, not fresh to sea water.
4. Mudskipper has NO measured multi-day maximum: 48 h tested, 2.5 days reported by a journalist, P. cantonensis "days" (number not seen). Use a floor.
5. Lungfish: wild 7-8 months, 4 yr review claim, 7 yr captive claim all REPORTED; no source for salinity lethality or heat; use "freshwater fish, no study shows it in sea water".
6. Heat round: pupfish 44.6 is a range endpoint (measured 42.7 C); goldfish range 30.8-43.6 C by acclimation; cod 22 C; salmon ~26 C indirect; eel is a coin flip (38 C upper lethal) so DROP eel as a fail; clownfish has no number so DROP or re-pull.
7. Icefish heat: Somero & DeVries 1967 was NOT icefish; use Chaenocephalus aceratus CTmax ~14 C (57 F), not 43 F/6 C.
8. Brown & Muir 1970 is J Fish Res Board Can, not JEB; great white stays ESTIMATED (anatomy), grey reef shark paper shows classes are moving.
9. Goldfish: no 24 h out-of-water study; lethal salinity 20 ppt abrupt (all dead); anoxia is a cold-water trick (16 h at 20 C vs months below 5 C), so do not pair it with the 100 F round.
10. Cameos: archerfish 65 cm/100% unsupported (use 20-60 cm trained targets, ~2 m reported); flying fish 45 s ok; sailfish 7 m/s burst vs 35 m/s old claim ok; pupfish 80-85 ppt unsourced (use 0-70 ppt); mummichog and tilapia not re-checked.
