# Game ideas

Candidates for future Sunday games, with the research behind each. Started October 27, 2026. Every game stays our own design in a genre or mechanic an old game made popular, never a clone of one game.

**A good candidate has:**
- players still missing it
- no good modern version, where a free version full of ads counts as none, because "no ads" is our pitch
- an original 2D browser version we could build in a week, with 10 chapters and a twist in each

**Already taken by our games:** Snake, marble shooter, eat-and-grow fish, mining, Heroes-style strategy, eat-to-grow crab.

**Status (Oct 27, 2026):**
- The user found the underserved-classics list below didn't resonate.
- The fake-ad formats are the direction under discussion.
- Nothing has been picked yet.

## 1. The real game from the fake ads (current direction)

Mobile games advertise puzzles that are barely in the game. Players install, feel cheated, and still want the game from the ad.

- **The real versions are free hypercasual games** full of ads. A search of the App Store (US) found no paid version of any format below in the top 100 results.
- **The only paid version is *Those Games*** (Monkeycraft and D3Publisher, $9.99, PC, Switch and PlayStation, with no phone or browser version). Each release packs 5 shallow minigames.
  - Steam reviews dropped from 1,277 (2023, 71% positive) to 181 (2024, 66%) to 45 (2025, 64%).
  - The A.V. Club praised its pin mode and called the rest "not robust enough".
  - So **one format per game, made deep with a twist every chapter,** is what's missing.

**Not worth it:**
- Parking escape and liquid sort: huge real genres, no fake-ad story.
- Kingshot's tower defence: Kingdom Rush covers it.
- Horde "mowing" survivors: Vampire Survivors covers it.
- The harvester upgrade loop: InkMine covers it.
- Merge Mansion and Lily's Garden story ads: no gameplay to make real.

**Why it fits:**
- Reviews of the free versions are full of "an ad every 5 seconds" and fake close buttons.
- Removing ads in them costs $2.99–$4.99, the same as our full game.
- Our promo shorts show the real game, which is the opposite of what got these ads banned.

**Search opening:** a search for "pull the pin game no ads" returns only App Store listings, and no web page answers "games that are actually like the ads". A page called "The pin puzzle from the ads, without the ads" would follow our "games like" pattern (see [seo-playbook.md](seo-playbook.md)).

**Risks:**
- The formats peaked around 2020–2022: still familiar from current ads, but not new.
- Free versions are one click away on Poki and CrazyGames, so depth has to carry the $4.99.
- Brand names (Hero Wars, Homescapes, Last War, Count Masters, Mob Control) may appear only in page text for comparison, never in our titles, art or ads.
- "Pull the Pin" carries a ™ claim by Popcore, so call ours a pin puzzle.

**Other attempts:**
- KFC UAE's free "Original Fake Games" app (2025): downloads 300% above target in its first week.
- "Real War (Not Fake)" (Nov 2025): a free parody of the Last War ads, with ads.

## 2. Underserved classics

Old games that are still loved and have no good modern version.

### Shortlist

| Idea | Why it's open | Fit and risks |
|---|---|---|
| **Insaniquarium-style tank defence** (PopCap 2001): feed pets that drop coins, buy more, fight raiders | The best evidence of the lot, confirmed by three agents separately. The original still sells on Steam, at 97% positive from about 4,900 reviews, with 45 new reviews in the last month. There's no sequel (PopCap's became Plants vs. Zombies), and only jam games and hobby projects play like it. | Yes: one screen, tap controls, 10 habitats each with a new pet and raider. Needs a non-fish setting (beehive, terrarium, cloud garden) to stay clear of InkFish. |
| **Contraption puzzles** (The Incredible Machine, 1993): place parts, press play | Nothing good on phones or in the browser. Contraption Maker (2014) is PC only, and recent rivals get 1–4 Steam reviews. Rube Goldberg drew his machines in pen and ink. | Yes: a new set of parts each chapter. Level design is the cost, and the physics needs a fixed time step so levels behave the same on every device. |
| **Diner Dash-style restaurant levels** (2004): line up actions, chain combos, beat expert scores | The loudest nostalgia: r/CozyGamers threads with 520 points (Jan 2025) and 118 points (Jan 2026, "nothing matches up"). The phone version is free-to-play with ads and gems. | Yes: 10 venues, with Cake Mania, Wedding Dash and Hotel Dash ideas as chapter twists. People pay little: GameHouse's Delicious: The First Course (Nov 2025, $19.99) has 12 Steam reviews. |
| **Rampart-style castles** (1990): wall in castles with Tetris-shaped pieces, then fire cannons | Its only rival, Battlements (Steam, July 2026), has 1 review. Wikipedia: 16K views a year. | Yes: drag and tap. Watch Battlements. |
| **Paperboy-style deliveries** (1985) | Nothing modern plays like it. Wikipedia: 63K views a year, the most of any open game. | Yes, but the scrolling street is a lot of art |
| **2D stealth with light, sound and distractions** (Mark of the Ninja, Thief) | Mark of the Ninja (2012) never got a sequel. The phone version, Hunter Assassin, only has guards' sight lines. | Yes: top-down, tap to move, a new tool or floor each chapter. Guard behaviour is the hardest code. |
| **Mummy Maze-style chase puzzles** (PopCap 2001): the chaser takes 2 steps for each of yours | The original is discontinued and was never on Steam. The rules come from Robert Abbott's free 1962 maze puzzles. | Yes, the easiest build: a solver can check each level. Not many people miss it. |
| **Bookworm Adventures-style word battles** (PopCap 2006) | Both originals have been delisted since 2016 | Yes, on phones. English only. PC rivals are arriving: Cursed Words (2026), Sternly Worded Adventures (2027). |

### What the other AIs suggested (Oct 27, 2026)

- **ChatGPT** (Diner Dash, Virtual Villagers, Chocolatier, Insaniquarium):
  - Its Reddit threads are real, but it overstated them: its "600+ upvotes" thread had 520, and a July 2026 post had 1 point.

  - Every genre it called abandoned had new releases in 2024–2026.

- **Perplexity** (Thief, RollerCoaster Tycoon, Sims 2, Age of Empires, KOTOR and others): mostly big games that already have modern versions.

## How to check an idea

- **Reddit:** blocked to search and fetch tools here. Arctic Shift, a public archive of Reddit posts with an open API, gives thread titles, dates and scores. One agent used it before the user had said whether that was OK, and the question is still open.
- **Demand without Reddit:**
  - Steam review counts for the original, including reviews in the last 30 days.
  - Flash-site play counts.
  - Yearly English Wikipedia page views, from the Wikimedia API: `wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/metrics/pageviews/per-article/en.wikipedia/all-access/user/<Article>/monthly/<start>/<end>`. These favour arcade classics.
- **Phone competition:** the iTunes Search API (`itunes.apple.com/search`) gives ratings and prices.
- **Search volumes:** the Ahrefs and Similarweb connectors would give them once authorized in claude.ai.
- **Web search limit:** all agents in one turn share 200 web searches. Run at most 2–3 research agents at a time.
- **Before building:** run a fact-check agent that tries to find a modern alternative, as for the SEO pages.
